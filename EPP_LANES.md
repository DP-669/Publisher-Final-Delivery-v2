## Why this file exists
Sub-publishers say EPP "does everything" and doesn't stick. The catalog already had the fix in 2022: a lane system ("Sounds Like Trouble", "Sounds Carefree", "Orchestral Drama"). It drifted — recent albums either bolted the lane onto the title ("Sounds Like Mystery - Questionable Things", 40+ characters) or dropped it entirely. This file makes the lanes explicit, keeps titles short, and gives sub-publishers a one-page map.

## The rule
- Every EPP album belongs to exactly one lane.
- The album title is 2–3 words and never contains the lane.
- The lane is the first keyword on every track and the first Fits tag.
- A new lane is created only when two albums need it. One album is not a lane.
- Lanes are proposed by the app from the analysis and confirmed by Damir in Tab 03.

## Sub-publisher one-pager (draft, company voice)
Ekonomic Propaganda is organised in lanes. Each lane is a consistent brief: same energy, same use. Search the lane name in SourceAudio and every track in it appears.

Sounds Like Trouble — dark hip-hop, swagger, sports and reality promos.
Sounds Like Mischief — playful orchestral dramedy, sneaky and light.
Sounds Like Mystery — tension without violence; investigation, reveal.
Sounds Carefree — bright, warm, acoustic to anthem; lifestyle and brand.
Sounds Tender — intimate, honest, small; documentary and drama.
Orchestral Drama — full orchestra, stakes, resolve; sports and human-interest.
